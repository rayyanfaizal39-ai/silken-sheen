## 4. FINDINGS

### CRITICAL

**C-01 · Saturn is taught as having weaker gravity than Earth, contradicting the figure shown beside it**
`interactive-bm.ts:81` and `:170`; `interactive-dlp.ts:81` and `:170` — **4 learner-facing occurrences, both streams.**
- Says: *"Zuhal … mempunyai graviti lebih lemah berbanding Bumi"* / *"Saturn … has weaker gravity than Earth"*.
- The same component displays **Zuhal 10.44 m s⁻²** and **Bumi 9.8 m s⁻²**. Saturn's gravity is **greater**.
- Source p.259 says gas giants' gravity is *"**tidak terlalu tinggi** berbanding dengan Bumi"* (not *too high*) — never *weaker*. Of the four gas giants only Uranus (8.69) is below Earth.
- This is scientifically wrong, self-contradictory on screen, and sits inside the card that teaches the DSKP-required density↔gravity relationship.

### HIGH

**H-01 · SP 12.1.2 — five of ten required comparison characteristics are absent, and there is no table**
DSKP requires saiz, jarak, suhu, ketumpatan, tarikan graviti *relatif kepada Bumi*, lapisan atmosfera, keadaan permukaan, arah **dan kelajuan** putaran, peredaran pada orbit, satelit semula jadi — and explicitly *"Membina **jadual**"*.
Present per planet: jarak, diameter, graviti, bulan, masa orbit (**5**).
Absent per planet: **suhu, ketumpatan, lapisan atmosfera, keadaan permukaan, halaju putaran** (all verified 0 occurrences). Gravity is given absolute, not relative to Earth. No tabular comparison exists — the data is 8 separate sphere pop-ups.

**H-02 · SP 12.1.4 — three of four DSKP prompts are missing entirely**
Covered: "Bumi berhenti berputar" (card, carrying Rajah 12.4's four printed effects correctly).
Missing from every surface: **"Mengapa terdapat planet yang mempunyai 2 bulan atau lebih?"**, **"terangkan pemerhatian anda tentang bentuk Bumi"** from the Moon, **"Adakah Bumi kelihatan mempunyai fasa?"** (0 hits for *fasa*/*phase* in interactive and mind map). The Moon card teaches tidal locking instead — related, but not the asked outcome.

**H-03 · Learner-facing section number "12.2" does not exist in the syllabus or textbook**
DSKP has exactly one SK (12.1). In the textbook "12.2" appears only as *Rajah 12.2*, *Jadual 12.2*, *Gambar foto 12.2* and *Aktiviti 12.2* — never as a subtopic heading. AcadeMY renders a second section numbered **12.2 Lapan Planet**, and the self-check heading reads **"Semak diri — 12.2"**, implying a second Standard Kandungan that does not exist.

**H-04 · Ecological footprint's six components are missing (SP 12.1.5)**
The definition is present and accurate. The six areas the textbook enumerates on p.263 — jejak karbon, kawasan binaan, hutan, kawasan pertanian, **kawasan penternakan**, kawasan perikanan — appear in **no Chapter 12 surface** (verified: 0 hits for *penternakan*/*grazing* across interactive, quizzes, flashcards, mind map). Also absent: *"Jejak ekologi berbeza daripada sebuah negara dengan negara yang lain"* and the DSKP Catatan's sustainable-living / individual-responsibility framing.

**H-05 · The BM stream shows an English calculator UI**
`src/components/notes/blocks/AuLightYearCalculator.tsx:17` hardcodes `Distance (km)` and `:33` hardcodes `Enter a distance`. The component accepts **no `lang` prop**. Confirmed in the browser: the BM stream renders **"DISTANCE (KM)"**. This is the only interactive for SP 12.1.1.

### MEDIUM

**M-01 · Quiz answer positions are badly skewed**
BM and DLP identical: index histogram `{0: 3, 1: 18, 2: 9, 3: 0}`. **Option D is never correct**, and 60 % of answers are option B. A student answering "B" throughout scores 18/30.

**M-02 · Quiz coverage is concentrated on SP 12.1.1**
Approx. distribution: **12.1.1 ≈ 16/30**, 12.1.2 ≈ 7, 12.1.3 ≈ 4, **12.1.4 = 1**, 12.1.5 ≈ 3. Difficulty is well balanced (10 Easy / 10 Medium / 10 Hard), but SP weighting is not.

**M-03 · SP 12.1.3's "anomali" is absent from the mind map, and 12.1.3 is absent from the reflection list**
Mind map: 0 hits for *anomali* / *paling panas*, 0 for *hipotetikal*/*berhenti berputar*, 0 for *fasa*. `reflectionItems` has **4 entries for 5 SPs** — the "meneroka hubungan … termasuk anomali" statement is missing, whereas the source's Refleksi Kendiri (p.266) carries all five.

**M-04 · The calculator contradicts its own instruction**
Instruction reads *"Cuba jarak Bumi-Matahari: 1.5 × 10⁸ km = 1.0 A.U."* but `defaultKm` is `149600000`, which the calculator renders as **9.97e-1 A.U.** A learner following the instruction does not see 1.0.

**M-05 · Planet spheres imply a relative size they do not honour, with no "not to scale" note**
Rendered size ratio Musytari : Utarid = **2.3×**; true diameter ratio = **29.3×**. No scale claim is made, so this is not a false statement — but the source's own Rajah 12.1 is explicitly captioned **"Tidak mengikut skala"**, and AcadeMY carries no equivalent (0 hits).

**M-06 · Unit conversion is one-way only**
DSKP 12.1.1 Catatan requires converting *"antara a.u. dan tahun cahaya **dan kilometer**"*. The calculator accepts km only and outputs A.U. + ly. There is no A.U. → km or ly → km path, although Sumatif Q3 (p.267) asks exactly that direction, and the chapter's own self-check poses it.

**M-07 · Chapter 12 has no regression tests**
Every other audited Form 2 chapter ships `chapter-N-remediation.test.tsx`. Chapter 12 ships none, so none of the above would be caught by CI.

### LOW

**L-01 · Calculator input is 38 px tall and unlabelled** — below the 44 px target at all four widths; `aria-label` is null.
**L-02 · Distance/temperature values follow Jadual 12.3/12.5, not Jadual 12.2** — Zuhal 1,427 (not 1,429), Neptun 4,497 (not 4,504), Venus 462 °C (not 457 °C). Internally consistent and traceable to the source, but it is the *secondary* table; worth a recorded decision since Jadual 12.2 is the chapter's headline comparison.
**L-03 · "Deimos" vs the printed "Demos"** — AcadeMY spells Mars's moon correctly; the textbook (p.254) misspells it. Divergence in the correct direction; record it so it is not "fixed" backwards.
**L-04 · Dead notes files** — `notes-bm.ts` / `notes-dlp.ts` (~12.6 k chars each) are registered but unreachable. Maintenance and drift risk.
**L-05 · Source oddities, no action required of AcadeMY** — the p.251 Kata Kunci list includes **"Amplitud"** (a Chapter 10 carry-over), and **Rajah 12.8 does not exist**.
