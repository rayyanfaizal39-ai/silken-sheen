# **AcadeMY Nova Companion — Master Art Direction Brief**

## **Professional Visual Specification**

### Version 1.0

---

## **1. Purpose**

The AcadeMY Nova Companion is a **premium magical collectible companion system** for students.\
Each companion is personalized based on the student’s answers, then evolves visually through learning progress.

The artwork must feel:

- **premium**
- **emotionally lovable**
- **high quality**
- **magical**
- **collectible**
- **recognizable across stages**
- **consistent with AcadeMY’s luxury navy-and-gold visual identity**

This is **not** a generic cartoon pet.\
This is a **signature magical companion brand asset**.

---

## **2. Core Creative Goal**

Create a companion image that feels like:

- a **beloved magical creature**
- a **rare collectible**
- a **personal learning companion**
- a **high-end fantasy illustration**
- a **premium digital card character**

The companion should create **instant first-impression delight**.

When a student sees their Nova, the reaction should be:

> “Wow, this is mine.”

The artwork is generated based on the student’s quiz results.

These inputs should shape the design:

- **name / username**
- **element**
- **personality archetype**
- **species class**
- **temperament**
- **colour palette**
- **environment / habitat**
- **visual motifs**
- **mood**
- **rarity**
- **special magical trait**
- **behavioral energy**

### Example personalization mapping

A student may produce:

- Element: Water
- Personality: Explorer
- Species: Magical Fox
- Mood: Playful & Mysterious
- Habitat: Moonlit Ocean

- Motif: Celestial markings

> a moonlit sapphire fox with gold celestial linework, crystal-like eyes, soft luminous fur, wave-like tail motion, and magical starlight accents.

---

## **7. Stage 1 Generation Rule**

### **Stage 1 = Origin**

At the first creation moment:

- Generate **2 different premium companion designs**
- Both must match the same student profile
- Both must clearly feel like the same profile identity
- The differences should be in:
  - pose
  - composition
  - expression
  - decorative details
  - mane / fur flow
  - magical accent arrangement
  - visual emphasis

### **Important**

The two options should **not** look almost identical.\
They must be meaningfully distinct while still clearly belonging to the same concept family.

### Student experience goal

The student should feel:

> “Both are beautiful… I need to choose my favorite.”

---

- 3/4 body
- full body

- slightly dynamic posture
- readable silhouette

### **Background**

### **Background rule**

The background must be:

- beautiful
- atmospheric
- premium
- supportive

But it must **not overpower the character**.

The companion remains the hero.

## **12. Evolution System Art Rules**

The Nova system uses **5 stages**:

1. **Origin**
2. **Awakened**
3. **Ascended**
4. **Mythic**
5. **Legendary**

### Core rule

Each evolution must remain clearly recognizable as the **same companion**.

The following must stay consistent:

- face identity
- core species identity
- silhouette family
- primary personality vibe
- defining visual motif

### What may evolve

- ornament complexity
- aura intensity
- magical detail richness
- costume / accessory refinement
- environmental grandeur
- tail / wing / mane elegance
- eye glow intensity
- power presence
- rarity presentation

### Evolution philosophy

Each stage should feel like:

> “the same beloved companion becoming more extraordinary.”

## **17. Technical Composition Guidance for AI Generation**

### Preferred outputs

- high resolution
- polished final illustration quality
- portrait orientation preferred for card compatibility
- full subject clarity
- readable on mobile and desktop
- face must be clear even when scaled down

### Avoid technical problems

- extra limbs
- distorted paws / tails / ears
- broken accessories
- muddy fur edges
- unreadable eyes
- cluttered composition
- too-dark subject
- overly busy background
- inconsistent anatomy
- duplicate decorative shapes
- random text inside the image

## **19. Master Prompt Structure**

Here is the **professional master prompt template**:

---

### **Nova Master Prompt Template**

**Create a premium cinematic fantasy illustration of a magical companion creature for AcadeMY.**

The companion is a personalized collectible learning companion for a student.

**Character Profile**

- Name: [insert name]
- Element: [insert element]
- Personality: [insert personality type]
- Species: [insert species]
- Temperament: [insert temperament]
- Habitat: [insert habitat]
- Colour palette: [insert palette]
- Special motifs: [insert motifs]
- Eye style: [insert eye style]
- Rarity: [insert rarity]
- Evolution stage: [insert stage]
- Description: [insert design summary]

**Art Direction**\
Create a highly detailed, polished, premium fantasy creature illustration with expressive eyes, luxurious texture detail, elegant magical ornaments, cinematic lighting, soft glow effects, celestial atmosphere, and collectible-card quality rendering.

The companion should feel lovable, magical, and premium.\
It must look like a rare personal magical creature designed specifically for a student.

Use a beautiful fantasy background that matches the habitat and element, but keep the character as the main focus.

The composition should be clean, striking, and emotionally appealing.\
Make the creature cute yet majestic, with a clear silhouette and strong face identity.

**Stage Consistency Requirement**\
This image must preserve the established identity of the companion if it is an evolved stage. Keep the same face, species identity, key colour family, and signature motifs, while making the design more refined and powerful according to the evolution stage.

**Visual Quality**

- ultra-detailed
- high-end fantasy illustration
- premium digital collectible style
- elegant lighting
- refined composition
- magical but not childish
- beautiful enough for a premium trading card

**Avoid**\
Do not include text, watermark, extra limbs, distorted anatomy, ugly expressions, flat cartoon style, horror aesthetics, cluttered layout, or low-detail rendering.

## **20. Stage-Specific Prompt Add-on**

### **Origin add-on**

Show the earliest beautiful form of the companion.\
Keep the design elegant and lovable, with clear identity and magical charm.

### **Awakened add-on**

Increase magical energy, ornament refinement, and aura presence while preserving the original face and identity.

### **Ascended add-on**

Show a stronger, more majestic, more refined version with evolved details and a more powerful visual presence.

### **Mythic add-on**

Create a rare and breathtaking version with advanced magical detail, elevated grandeur, and premium celestial power.

### **Legendary add-on**

Create the ultimate masterpiece version of the same companion, fully realized, majestic, iconic, and visually unforgettable.

---

## **21. Stage 1 Dual-Choice Prompt Add-on**

When generating the initial two options:

> Generate **two distinct design variations** of the same companion concept, both based on the exact same student profile.\
> Both designs must be beautiful, premium, and clearly related, but different enough in pose, expression, visual emphasis, ornament detail, and composition so the student can meaningfully choose one favorite.
