- **No fixed refrigerated-match observation was verified.** The question remains open. The diagram deliberately does not indicate that either match lights or fails to light. This is not an experiment result.

- BM and DLP apparatus wording differs for the oxygen ignition device and stored-match location. Preserve those labels without changing scientific geometry.
- The inspected BM answer-key extraction does not include Formative 7.2 question 5; DLP explicitly does. The requested narrow concept is supported by that verified source and the user's direction.
