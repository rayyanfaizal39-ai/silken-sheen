## Source gap and qualifications

- Formative Practice 7.1 question 2 asks how to identify oxygen and carbon dioxide. Pages 196–203 do not supply the test procedures, and the checked answer-key entry does not give an answer for question 2. It remains an open question. No glowing-splint/limewater answer was imported from general knowledge.
- The figure/prose distinction for the 0.97% label is recorded above. Variable components receive no numerical allocation.
- Apparatus, gas-use icons and cycle node illustrations are schematic. Their geometry and numbering are presentation mappings, not additional apparatus readings or biological facts.
- Non-literal presentation copy added: the user-requested Before / During / After (Sebelum / Semasa / Selepas) controls; original/final water-level callouts; paired 21% composition and 1/5 ≈ 20% activity labels. These express the audited figures and the user's explicit comparison request.
- **Unsourced scientific facts added: none.** No source ambiguity was resolved using outside scientific knowledge.
