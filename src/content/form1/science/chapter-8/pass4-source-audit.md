BM labels the parts **A/B**; DLP labels them **I/II**. Both are preserved while sharing exactly the same apparatus geometry.

The printed BM apparatus list is **Prisma kaca, skrin putih, kotak sinar, cermin, gelas kaca**. It omits several items explicitly required by its own Part B instructions and Figure 8.20. The DLP list includes the prism, white screen, ray box, plane mirror, water, torchlight, white paper, basin, tape and round black cardboard. Preserve each printed list rather than silently substituting one edition's list for the other. The BM Part B setup uses the basin and other items actually specified in its instructions/figure. No invented use for the BM list's glass cup is added. DLP calls the card round; the common schematic shows a card at the torch aperture. BM requires a central hole and explicitly mentions tape at the torch; DLP's step 3 says attach the card without repeating that tape instruction. Each edition retains its own procedure.
