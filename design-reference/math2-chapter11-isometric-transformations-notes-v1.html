Isometric TransformationsTransformasi Isometri

Press your palm onto paper, lift it, press again beside the first print. Same hand shape, same size — just moved. That's a transformation: a shape moving, without changing what it actually is. Tekan tapak tangan anda ke kertas, angkat, tekan semula sebelah cetakan pertama. Bentuk tangan sama, saiz sama — cuma berpindah. Itulah transformasi: bentuk bergerak, tanpa mengubah apa ia sebenarnya.

11.1

TransformationsTransformasi

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A transformation moves every point of a shape (the object) the same way to create a new copy (the image) — without changing the shape itself. If the shape changes, it's NOT a transformation. Transformasi mengalihkan setiap titik bentuk (objek) cara sama untuk cipta salinan baharu (imej) — tanpa mengubah bentuk itu sendiri. Jika bentuk berubah, itu BUKAN transformasi.

CongruenceKekongruenan
Same shape + same size = congruent (orientation doesn't matter)Bentuk sama + saiz sama = kongruen (orientasi tidak penting)
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
ABCDEF is an object, PQRSTU is its image. State the image of (a) point C (b) line AB.ABCDEF ialah objek, PQRSTU ialah imejnya. Nyatakan imej bagi (a) titik C (b) garis AB.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Two 10 sen coins — are they congruent? What about a 10 sen and a 20 sen coin?Dua syiling 10 sen — adakah kongruen? Bagaimana pula syiling 10 sen dan 20 sen?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Two 10 sen coins: congruent (same shape and size). A 10 sen and 20 sen: same shape (round) but different size — similar, not congruent. Dua syiling 10 sen: kongruen (bentuk dan saiz sama). 10 sen dan 20 sen: bentuk sama (bulat) tetapi saiz berbeza — serupa, bukan kongruen.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Assuming a flipped or rotated shape can't be congruent to the original.Menganggap bentuk dibalik atau diputar tidak boleh kongruen dengan asal.
✅Congruence only cares about shape and size — orientation (which way it's facing) doesn't matter at all.Kekongruenan hanya peduli bentuk dan saiz — orientasi (arah menghadap) langsung tidak penting.

Explain why a shadow of an object on a wall, changing size as the light source moves closer or further, is NOT a transformation of the object.Jelaskan mengapa bayang objek di dinding, berubah saiz apabila sumber cahaya bergerak lebih dekat atau jauh, BUKAN transformasi objek.
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan

11.2

TranslationTranslasi

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A translation slides every point the SAME direction and SAME distance — nothing rotates, nothing flips. It's written as a vector: how far sideways, how far up/down. Translasi menggelongsor setiap titik arah SAMA dan jarak SAMA — tiada apa berputar, tiada apa terbalik. Ditulis sebagai vektor: berapa jauh ke sisi, berapa jauh atas/bawah.

Vector NotationTatatanda Vektor
(a, b)  or written as a column: [a above b]
a= sideways (+right/−left),= ke sisi (+kanan/−kiri), b= up/down (+up/−down)= atas/bawah (+atas/−bawah)
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Point A(1,2) maps to A'(3,6) under a translation. State the translation vector.Titik A(1,2) memetakan A'(3,6) bawah translasi. Nyatakan vektor translasi.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Point L(1,4) maps to L'(3,−5). Using the same translation, find the image of A(3,1).Titik L(1,4) memetakan L'(3,−5). Guna translasi sama, cari imej A(3,1).

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Vector = (3−1, −5−4) = (2,−9). A'=(3+2, 1−9)=(5,−8).
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Mixing up which number in the vector is sideways and which is up/down.Mengelirukan nombor mana dalam vektor ke sisi dan mana atas/bawah.
✅Always the SAME order: sideways first, then up/down — just like (x,y) coordinates.Sentiasa susunan SAMA: ke sisi dahulu, kemudian atas/bawah — sama seperti koordinat (x,y).
✏️ Uji DiriUji Diri

Under a translation, S'(4,−2) is the image of S. If the vector is (1,4), find the coordinates of S (the original object).Bawah translasi, S'(4,−2) ialah imej S. Jika vektor (1,4), cari koordinat S (objek asal).
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan

11.3

ReflectionPantulan

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Look in a mirror — your reflection is the same distance behind the glass as you are in front of it. A reflection works the same way: every point flips across a line (the axis of reflection), landing the SAME distance on the other side. Lihat dalam cermin — pantulan anda jarak sama di belakang kaca seperti anda di depannya. Pantulan berfungsi cara sama: setiap titik terbalik merentasi garis (paksi pantulan), mendarat jarak SAMA di sebelah lain.

Object and image are the same shape, same size, MIRROR-flipped orientationObjek dan imej bentuk sama, saiz sama, orientasi TERBALIK cermin
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Triangle M reflects to M'. Describe the reflection if M' appears directly below M, flipped vertically.Segi tiga M pantul ke M'. Terangkan pantulan jika M' muncul terus di bawah M, terbalik menegak.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Point (3,4) is reflected in the x-axis. Find its image.Titik (3,4) dipantul dalam paksi-x. Cari imejnya.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Reflecting in the x-axis flips the y-value's sign: (3,−4). Memantul dalam paksi-x membalikkan tanda nilai-y: (3,−4).
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Confusing reflection with rotation — both can look similar in a quick sketch.Mengelirukan pantulan dengan putaran — kedua-dua boleh nampak serupa dalam lakaran pantas.
✅Reflection ALWAYS mirror-flips the orientation. Rotation keeps the same "handedness" — check whether the shape looks mirrored or just turned.Pantulan SENTIASA membalikkan orientasi seperti cermin. Putaran kekalkan "kepihakan" sama — semak sama ada bentuk nampak dicermin atau hanya dipusing.
✏️ Uji DiriUji Diri

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan

11.4

RotationPutaran

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A clock's hands sweep around a fixed point — the centre of rotation — without changing size. A rotation needs three things to describe it fully: the centre, the angle, and the direction (clockwise or anticlockwise). Jarum jam mengelilingi titik tetap — pusat putaran — tanpa mengubah saiz. Putaran perlukan tiga perkara untuk terangkan sepenuhnya: pusat, sudut, dan arah (ikut jam atau lawan jam).

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Describe the rotation that maps triangle ABCD to A'B'C'D' if it turns 90° in the clockwise direction around point T.Terangkan putaran yang memetakan segi tiga ABCD ke A'B'C'D' jika berpusing 90° arah ikut jam sekeliling titik T.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Is a rotation of 180° clockwise the same as 180° anticlockwise?Adakah putaran 180° ikut jam sama dengan 180° lawan jam?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Yes — turning exactly halfway around lands in the same spot either direction. Ya — berpusing tepat separuh jalan mendarat di tempat sama tidak kira arah.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Describing a rotation without stating the centre — the description is incomplete.Terangkan putaran tanpa nyatakan pusat — penerangan tidak lengkap.
✅Every full rotation description needs all THREE: centre, angle, AND direction.Setiap penerangan putaran penuh perlukan KETIGA-TIGA: pusat, sudut, DAN arah.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan

11.5

Isometry and Rotational SymmetryIsometri dan Simetri Putaran

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Translation, reflection, and rotation are all isometries — they keep every distance between points exactly the same, which means the object and image are always CONGRUENT. Translasi, pantulan, dan putaran semuanya isometri — ia kekalkan setiap jarak antara titik tepat sama, bermakna objek dan imej sentiasa KONGRUEN.

A shape has rotational symmetry if, when rotated less than a full turn around a fixed point, it looks EXACTLY the same as it started. Bentuk ada simetri putaran jika, apabila diputar kurang daripada satu pusingan penuh sekeliling titik tetap, ia nampak TEPAT sama seperti permulaan.

Order of Rotational SymmetryPeringkat Simetri Putaran
How many times it looks the same in one full 360° turnBerapa kali ia nampak sama dalam satu pusingan penuh 360°
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Objects A, B, C, D are all congruent to each other. State the isometry that maps A to B (a rotation), A to C (a translation), and A to D (a reflection).Objek A, B, C, D semuanya kongruen antara satu sama lain. Nyatakan isometri yang memetakan A ke B (putaran), A ke C (translasi), dan A ke D (pantulan).
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

What is the order of rotational symmetry of an equilateral triangle?Apakah peringkat simetri putaran segi tiga sama sisi?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
3 — it looks identical every 120° turn (360°÷3). 3 — ia nampak sama setiap pusingan 120° (360°÷3).
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Assuming every congruent pair must be a translation.Menganggap setiap pasangan kongruen mesti translasi.
✅Congruent just means "isometry happened" — check the orientation carefully to identify WHICH isometry (translation keeps facing the same way, rotation turns, reflection mirrors).Kongruen hanya bermaksud "isometri berlaku" — semak orientasi dengan teliti untuk kenal pasti isometri MANA (translasi kekal menghadap sama, putaran berpusing, pantulan mencermin).
✏️ Uji DiriUji Diri

A logo is designed with rotational symmetry of order 6 around its centre. If one "arm" of the logo starts pointing due north, at what other compass-like angles (measuring clockwise from north) will an identical arm appear? Logo direka dengan simetri putaran peringkat 6 sekeliling pusatnya. Jika satu "lengan" logo bermula menghala utara, pada sudut seperti kompas lain (mengukur ikut jam dari utara) lengan serupa akan muncul?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
