CoordinatesKoordinat

Walking from school to a friend's house, you move sideways then upwards — a hidden right-angled triangle. The straight-line "as the crow flies" distance is its hypotenuse, and Pythagoras already taught you how to find that. Berjalan dari sekolah ke rumah kawan, anda bergerak ke sisi kemudian ke atas — segi tiga bersudut tegak tersembunyi. Jarak garis lurus "seperti burung terbang" ialah hipotenusnya, dan Pythagoras sudah ajar anda cara mencarinya.

7.1

Distance in a Cartesian Coordinate SystemJarak dalam Sistem Koordinat Cartes

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Between any two points, draw a right-angled triangle — sideways then up. The straight-line distance between the points is the HYPOTENUSE of that triangle, so Pythagoras finds it directly. Antara sebarang dua titik, lukis segi tiga bersudut tegak — ke sisi kemudian ke atas. Jarak garis lurus antara titik ialah HIPOTENUS segi tiga itu, jadi Pythagoras mencarinya terus.

Distance FormulaFormula Jarak
d = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²]
This is just Pythagoras — (x₂−x₁) is the horizontal leg, (y₂−y₁) is the vertical legIni cuma Pythagoras — (x₂−x₁) kaki mendatar, (y₂−y₁) kaki menegak
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Find the distance between A(1,7) and B(5,1).Cari jarak antara A(1,7) dan B(5,1).
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Find the distance between P(−2,6) and Q(4,1).Cari jarak antara P(−2,6) dan Q(4,1).

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
d=√[(4−(−2))²+(1−6)²]=√[6²+(−5)²]=√61≈7.81 units. d=√[(4−(−2))²+(1−6)²]=√[6²+(−5)²]=√61≈7.81 unit.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Mixing up which point is (x₁,y₁) and which is (x₂,y₂), or subtracting in the wrong order.Mengelirukan titik mana (x₁,y₁) dan mana (x₂,y₂), atau menolak salah susunan.
✅It doesn't matter which point you call 1 or 2 — squaring removes any negative sign either way.Tidak penting titik mana anda panggil 1 atau 2 — kuasa dua buang sebarang tanda negatif kedua-dua cara.

7.2

Midpoint in the Cartesian Coordinate SystemTitik Tengah dalam Sistem Koordinat Cartes

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

The midpoint sits exactly halfway between two points. Its x-coordinate is the AVERAGE of the two x's, and its y-coordinate is the average of the two y's. Titik tengah terletak tepat separuh jalan antara dua titik. Koordinat-x ialah PURATA dua x, dan koordinat-y ialah purata dua y.

Midpoint FormulaFormula Titik Tengah
M = ( (x₁+x₂)/2 , (y₁+y₂)/2 )
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Find the midpoint of A(2,5) and B(2,1).Cari titik tengah A(2,5) dan B(2,1).
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Find the midpoint of M(10,7) and N(4,1).Cari titik tengah M(10,7) dan N(4,1).

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
((10+4)/2, (7+1)/2) = (7, 4).
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Subtracting instead of adding the coordinates before dividing by 2.Menolak bukan menambah koordinat sebelum bahagi dengan 2.
✅Midpoint is an AVERAGE — always add the two values, then divide by 2. Subtracting finds a distance, not a midpoint.Titik tengah ialah PURATA — sentiasa tambah dua nilai, kemudian bahagi 2. Menolak cari jarak, bukan titik tengah.

7.3

The Cartesian Coordinate SystemSistem Koordinat Cartes

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Many problems need BOTH the distance formula and the midpoint formula together — read the problem carefully to see which one (or both) it's asking for. Banyak masalah perlukan KEDUA-DUA formula jarak dan titik tengah bersama — baca masalah dengan teliti untuk lihat mana satu (atau kedua-dua) yang ditanya.

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
P(2,3) and Q(8,11) are two points. Find (a) the midpoint of PQ, (b) the distance PQ.P(2,3) dan Q(8,11) dua titik. Cari (a) titik tengah PQ, (b) jarak PQ.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

A(1,2) and B(7,2) are opposite ends of a diameter. Find the centre and radius of the circle.A(1,2) dan B(7,2) hujung bertentangan diameter. Cari pusat dan jejari bulatan.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Centre = midpoint = (4,2). Diameter = distance AB = 6. Radius = 3. Pusat = titik tengah = (4,2). Diameter = jarak AB = 6. Jejari = 3.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Using the midpoint formula when the question actually asks for distance, or vice versa.Guna formula titik tengah bila soalan sebenarnya tanya jarak, atau sebaliknya.
✅"Halfway" or "centre" → midpoint. "How far" or "length" → distance."Separuh jalan" atau "pusat" → titik tengah. "Berapa jauh" atau "panjang" → jarak.
