Ratios, Rates and ProportionsNisbah, Kadar dan Kadaran

A nurse counting a pulse. An astronomer comparing distances in the solar system. Every field that compares quantities relies on the same three ideas: ratios, rates, and proportions. Jururawat mengira nadi. Ahli astronomi membanding jarak dalam sistem suria. Setiap bidang yang membanding kuantiti bergantung pada tiga idea yang sama: nisbah, kadar, dan kadaran.

4.1

RatiosNisbah

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A ratio compares two or more quantities of the SAME kind, measured in the SAME unit. A ratio has no units — it only compares. For example, 5000 g to 9 kg: convert to the same unit first (5 kg : 9 kg), then drop the units → 5 : 9. Nisbah membanding dua atau lebih kuantiti JENIS SAMA, diukur dalam UNIT SAMA. Nisbah tiada unit — ia hanya membanding. Contohnya, 5000 g kepada 9 kg: tukar kepada unit sama dahulu (5 kg : 9 kg), kemudian gugurkan unit → 5 : 9.

Ratios like 1:2, 2:4, and 6:12 are equivalent ratios — multiplying or dividing every part by the same number never changes what the ratio represents. Nisbah seperti 1:2, 2:4, dan 6:12 ialah nisbah setara — mendarab atau membahagi setiap bahagian dengan nombor sama tidak mengubah apa yang diwakili nisbah itu.

Simplifying a Ratio — Divide by the HCFMemudahkan Nisbah — Bahagi dengan FSTB
Just like Chapter 2, find the HCF of all parts using a division ladder, then divide every part by it.Seperti Bab 2, cari FSTB semua bahagian menggunakan tangga pembahagian, kemudian bahagi setiap bahagian dengannya.

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu

Express 800 g : 1.8 kg in its simplest form. Think: what must happen before you can even compare these two numbers?Ungkapkan 800 g : 1.8 kg dalam bentuk termudah. Fikir: apa perlu berlaku sebelum anda boleh banding kedua-dua nombor ini?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Convert to the same unit first: 1.8 kg = 1800 g. So 800 : 1800. HCF of 800 and 1800 is 200. Divide: 4 : 9. Tukar kepada unit sama dahulu: 1.8 kg = 1800 g. Jadi 800 : 1800. FSTB bagi 800 dan 1800 ialah 200. Bahagi: 4 : 9.

✅ALWAYS convert to the same unit BEFORE finding the HCF or simplifying.SENTIASA tukar kepada unit sama SEBELUM mencari FSTB atau memudahkan.

4.2

RatesKadar

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A rate is a special ratio that compares two quantities with DIFFERENT units — like 285 km in 3 hours, or RM10 for 2 kg. Ordinary ratios need matching units; rates are built on the fact that the units are different. Kadar ialah nisbah khas yang membanding dua kuantiti dengan unit BERBEZA — seperti 285 km dalam 3 jam, atau RM10 untuk 2 kg. Nisbah biasa perlukan unit sepadan; kadar dibina atas hakikat unit adalah berbeza.

Rate NotationTatatanda Kadar
285 km / 3 hours
Two DIFFERENT units, always written with a slash or "per"Dua unit BERBEZA, sentiasa ditulis dengan garis miring atau "setiap"
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Rajan is riding his bicycle at a speed of 5 m/s. Convert 5 m/s to km/h.Rajan menunggang basikal pada kelajuan 5 m/s. Tukar 5 m/s kepada km/j.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Convert Stall A to per kg: RM8/500g × 2 = RM16/kg. RM16 > RM15, so Stall B is cheaper. Tukar Gerai A kepada per kg: RM8/500g × 2 = RM16/kg. RM16 > RM15, jadi Gerai B lebih murah.

✅Always convert BOTH rates to the SAME unit before comparing which is cheaper/faster.Sentiasa tukar KEDUA-DUA kadar kepada unit SAMA sebelum banding mana lebih murah/laju.

4.3

ProportionsKadaran

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A proportion states that two ratios or two rates are EQUAL. For example, if 45 words take 5 minutes, then 540 words take 60 minutes — both sides describe the same rate, just scaled up. Kadaran menyatakan bahawa dua nisbah atau dua kadar adalah SAMA. Contohnya, jika 45 patah perkataan mengambil 5 minit, maka 540 patah perkataan mengambil 60 minit — kedua-dua belah menggambarkan kadar sama, hanya diskalakan.

Cross MultiplicationPendaraban Silang
a/b = c/d   ⟹   a × d = b × c
Multiply diagonally across the equals sign to solve for an unknownDarab secara pepenjuru merentasi tanda sama untuk selesaikan yang tidak diketahui
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Electricity costs 43.6 sen for 2 kWh. How much does 30 kWh cost?Elektrik berharga 43.6 sen bagi 2 kWj. Berapakah kos 30 kWj?
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

A gear rotates: when a large gear turns 4 times, a small gear turns 18 times. If the small gear turns 54 times, how many times does the large gear turn?Gear berputar: apabila gear besar berputar 4 kali, gear kecil berputar 18 kali. Jika gear kecil berputar 54 kali, berapa kali gear besar berputar?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
4/18 = x/54. Cross multiply: 18x = 4×54 = 216. x = 12. 4/18 = x/54. Darab silang: 18x = 4×54 = 216. x = 12.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Setting up the proportion with mismatched quantities on top and bottom (e.g. time over cost on one side, cost over time on the other).Menyediakan kadaran dengan kuantiti tidak sepadan di atas dan bawah.
✅Keep the SAME quantity type in the SAME position (top/bottom) on both sides of the proportion.Kekalkan JENIS kuantiti SAMA pada kedudukan SAMA (atas/bawah) pada kedua-dua belah kadaran.

4.4

Ratios, Rates and ProportionsNisbah, Kadar dan Kadaran

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

When you know p:q and q:r separately, you can chain them into one ratio p:q:r — but only if the shared term (q) is matched to the SAME value first. That's the LCM trick from Chapter 2, reused here. Apabila anda tahu p:q dan q:r berasingan, anda boleh gabungkannya kepada satu nisbah p:q:r — tetapi hanya jika sebutan dikongsi (q) disamakan kepada nilai SAMA dahulu. Itulah helah GSTK daripada Bab 2, digunakan semula di sini.

Chaining Ratios via the Shared TermMenggabungkan Nisbah melalui Sebutan Dikongsi
p : q = 7 : 3  &  q : r = 3 : 5  ⟹  p : q : r = 7 : 3 : 5
Here q is already 3 in both — no scaling needed. When it's NOT already equal, find the LCM of the two q-values first.Di sini q sudah 3 dalam kedua-dua — tiada penskalaan diperlukan. Apabila ia TIDAK sama, cari GSTK bagi kedua-dua nilai q dahulu.
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
The ratio of storybooks to reference books is 2:5. The ratio of reference books to magazines is 3:2. Find storybooks : reference books : magazines.Nisbah buku cerita kepada buku rujukan ialah 2:5. Nisbah buku rujukan kepada majalah ialah 3:2. Cari buku cerita : buku rujukan : majalah.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

The ratio of flour to water is 5:3. If there are 480 g of flour, how much water is needed?Nisbah tepung kepada air ialah 5:3. Jika ada 480 g tepung, berapa air diperlukan?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
5 parts = 480 g, so 1 part = 96 g. Water = 3 parts = 3 × 96 = 288 g. 5 bahagian = 480 g, jadi 1 bahagian = 96 g. Air = 3 bahagian = 3 × 96 = 288 g.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Combining p:q=2:5 and q:r=3:2 directly as p:q:r = 2:5:2 without matching q first.Menggabungkan p:q=2:5 dan q:r=3:2 terus sebagai p:q:r = 2:5:2 tanpa menyamakan q dahulu.
✅Scale BOTH ratios so the shared term (q) becomes the LCM of 5 and 3 (which is 15), then combine.Skalakan KEDUA-DUA nisbah supaya sebutan dikongsi (q) menjadi GSTK bagi 5 dan 3 (iaitu 15), kemudian gabungkan.
✏️ Uji DiriUji Diri

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
q is already 9 in both — p:q:r = 2:9:7.q sudah 9 dalam kedua-dua — p:q:r = 2:9:7.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan

4.5

Relationship between Ratios, Rates, Proportions, Percentages, Fractions and DecimalsPerhubungan antara Nisbah, Kadar, Kadaran, Peratusan, Pecahan dan Perpuluhan

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A percentage is just a ratio out of 100 — "percentage" literally means "per 100". So 20% = 20:100 = 20/100 = 0.2. Every percentage can be rewritten as a ratio, a fraction, or a decimal — they're four ways of saying the same thing. Peratusan hanyalah nisbah daripada 100 — "peratusan" bermaksud "per 100". Jadi 20% = 20:100 = 20/100 = 0.2. Setiap peratusan boleh ditulis semula sebagai nisbah, pecahan, atau perpuluhan — empat cara menyatakan perkara sama.

Percentage = Ratio out of 100Peratusan = Nisbah daripada 100
20% = 20 : 100 = 20/100 = 0.2
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
In a class, the ratio of girls to boys is 3:2. Find the percentage of girls in the class.Dalam kelas, nisbah pelajar perempuan kepada lelaki ialah 3:2. Cari peratusan pelajar perempuan.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Zakaria saves 30% of his daily pocket money. Find the ratio of his savings to his total pocket money.Zakaria simpan 30% wang saku hariannya. Cari nisbah simpanannya kepada jumlah wang saku.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
30% = 30/100 = 30:100. Simplify (HCF=10): 3:10. 30% = 30/100 = 30:100. Permudahkan (FSTB=10): 3:10.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Writing a ratio 3:2 (girls:boys) as "3% girls" without first finding girls-to-TOTAL.Menulis nisbah 3:2 (perempuan:lelaki) sebagai "3% perempuan" tanpa cari perempuan-kepada-JUMLAH dahulu.
✅A percentage of the WHOLE needs girls : TOTAL (3:5), not girls : boys (3:2).Peratusan daripada KESELURUHAN perlukan perempuan : JUMLAH (3:5), bukan perempuan : lelaki (3:2).
✏️ Uji DiriUji Diri
