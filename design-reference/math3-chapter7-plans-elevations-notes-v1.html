Plans and ElevationsPelan dan Dongakan

A drone photo taken straight down from above shows a building's true shape from the top — no distortion, no angle. That "straight down" view is exactly what a plan is, and architects use three of these views to describe any 3D object completely. Foto dron diambil terus dari atas tunjuk bentuk sebenar bangunan dari atas — tiada herotan, tiada sudut. Pandangan "terus ke bawah" itu tepat apa pelan, dan arkitek guna tiga pandangan ini untuk terangkan sebarang objek 3D sepenuhnya.

7.1

Orthogonal ProjectionsUnjuran Ortogon

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Drop a straight line STRAIGHT DOWN from every point of an object onto a flat surface — each line must hit the surface at a perfect right angle (a normal). The outline that lands on the surface is the object's orthogonal projection. Jatuhkan garis lurus TERUS KE BAWAH dari setiap titik objek ke permukaan rata — setiap garis mesti kena permukaan pada sudut tegak sempurna (normal). Garis besar yang mendarat pada permukaan ialah unjuran ortogon objek.

Normal to a PlaneNormal kepada Satah
A normal is perpendicular (90°) to every line on that planeNormal serenjang (90°) dengan setiap garis pada satah itu
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
A cuboid is projected onto a horizontal plane below it, with normals PA, QB, RC, SD. What is the orthogonal projection?Kuboid diunjur ke satah mendatar di bawahnya, dengan normal PA, QB, RC, SD. Apakah unjuran ortogon?
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

A line is projected onto a plane using a line that meets the plane at 60° instead of 90°. Is this a valid orthogonal projection?Garis diunjur ke satah guna garis bertemu satah pada 60° bukan 90°. Adakah ini unjuran ortogon sah?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
No — an orthogonal projection requires the projecting line to be a NORMAL, meeting the plane at exactly 90°. Tidak — unjuran ortogon perlukan garis unjuran menjadi NORMAL, bertemu satah pada tepat 90°.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Assuming any straight line dropped from the object to the plane counts as a valid projection.Menganggap sebarang garis lurus dijatuhkan dari objek ke satah dikira unjuran sah.
✅It only counts if that line is a NORMAL — perpendicular to the plane. A slanted line gives a distorted, non-orthogonal shadow.Ia hanya dikira jika garis itu NORMAL — serenjang dengan satah. Garis condong beri bayang herot, bukan ortogon.

7.2

Plans and ElevationsPelan dan Dongakan

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Any 3D object can be fully described with just THREE flat drawings: the plan (view from directly above), the front elevation (view from the front), and the side elevation (view from the side). Solid lines show edges you can see; dashed lines show edges hidden behind the object. Sebarang objek 3D boleh diterangkan sepenuhnya dengan hanya TIGA lukisan rata: pelan (pandangan dari terus atas), dongakan depan (pandangan dari depan), dan dongakan sisi (pandangan dari sisi). Garis pejal tunjuk tepi boleh dilihat; garis putus-putus tunjuk tepi tersembunyi di belakang objek.

Drawing to ScaleMelukis Berskala
Every plan and elevation is drawn to FULL SCALE — the actual sizeSetiap pelan dan dongakan dilukis SKALA PENUH — saiz sebenar
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
A cuboid box is 5cm long, 3cm wide, 2cm tall. Sketch (a) the plan (top view), (b) the front elevation, stating their dimensions.Kotak kuboid 5cm panjang, 3cm lebar, 2cm tinggi. Lakar (a) pelan (pandangan atas), (b) dongakan depan, nyatakan dimensinya.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

When drawing a side elevation, an edge exists but is completely hidden behind the front of the object. How should that edge be drawn?Bila melukis dongakan sisi, tepi wujud tetapi tersembunyi sepenuhnya di belakang depan objek. Bagaimana tepi itu patut dilukis?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
As a dashed line — hidden edges are always drawn dashed, never solid. Sebagai garis putus-putus — tepi tersembunyi sentiasa dilukis putus-putus, tidak pernah pejal.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Drawing the plan and elevations at different sizes based on "how it looks".Melukis pelan dan dongakan pada saiz berbeza berdasarkan "cara ia nampak".
✅Every view must use the SAME full scale — matching measurements should line up exactly between the plan and each elevation.Setiap pandangan mesti guna skala penuh SAMA — ukuran sepadan patut sejajar tepat antara pelan dan setiap dongakan.

Challenge MissionMisi Cabaran

🏆 KBAT

An L-shaped block is made of two cuboids joined together. Explain how many DIFFERENT elevations you would need to draw if you viewed it from all 4 sides (front, back, left, right), and why some might look identical. Blok berbentuk L dibuat daripada dua kuboid disambung. Jelaskan berapa dongakan BERBEZA anda perlu lukis jika anda pandang dari kesemua 4 sisi (depan, belakang, kiri, kanan), dan kenapa sesetengah mungkin nampak serupa.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
An L-shape typically needs 2 distinct elevations (front/back may differ from left/right) since front and back show mirror-image outlines that might look the same if the shape is symmetric along that axis — always check the actual geometry rather than assuming. Bentuk L biasanya perlukan 2 dongakan berbeza (depan/belakang mungkin berbeza daripada kiri/kanan) kerana depan dan belakang tunjuk garis besar bayangan cermin — sentiasa semak geometri sebenar bukan anggap sahaja.
