Diversity of Organisms

What is biodiversity?

Malaysia sits in an equatorial climate — warm, wet, and steady all year — which makes it one of only 12 megabiodiverse countries on Earth. Biodiversity simply means how varied life is: every microorganism, animal and plant that has adapted, over time, to survive in its own patch of habitat.

Beyond the basicsBiodiversity even reaches down to the genetic level — variation in the genes within a single species, not just between species.

Keeping biodiversity alive

Deforestation for timber and development puts biodiversity at risk. Malaysia manages this through law, protected space, and active breeding programmes.

⚖️ Legal protection▾

The Wildlife Protection Act 1972 bans the killing or trade of endemic and endangered species.

🏞️ Protected habitats▾

National parks, marine parks, forest reserves and wildlife sanctuaries give species room to live undisturbed.

🐢 Active recovery▾

Seedling nurseries and turtle hatcheries actively rebuild populations that have been pushed toward extinction.

Classification of Organisms

Why sort living things at all?

Animals and plants can be grouped into smaller and smaller sets based on shared and differing traits — this is what lets a biologist identify an unfamiliar organism quickly and consistently.
