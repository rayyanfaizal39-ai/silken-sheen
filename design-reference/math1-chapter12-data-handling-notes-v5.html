Data HandlingPengendalian Data

Malaysia's population is 30.6 million, made up of several ethnic groups in different proportions. Every news headline built on numbers follows the same five steps: ask a question, collect data, classify it, represent it, and interpret it. Populasi Malaysia ialah 30.6 juta, terdiri daripada beberapa kumpulan etnik dalam nisbah berbeza. Setiap tajuk berita dibina atas nombor mengikuti lima langkah sama: tanya soalan, kumpul data, klasifikasikan, wakilkan, dan tafsirkan.

12.1

Data Collection, Organization and Representation Process, and Interpretation of Data RepresentationProses Pengumpulan, Penyusunan dan Perwakilan Data, dan Pentafsiran Perwakilan Data

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A statistical question expects VARIED answers when data is collected (e.g. "How tall are the students in class?"), unlike a question with one fixed answer. Data collected splits into categorical (described, not measured — e.g. blood group) and numerical (measured — e.g. height), and numerical data is further either discrete (whole-unit counts) or continuous (measured on a scale). Soalan statistik mengharapkan jawapan BERBEZA-BEZA apabila data dikumpul (cth. "Berapa tinggi pelajar dalam kelas?"), berbeza dengan soalan berjawapan tunggal tetap. Data dikumpul terbahagi kepada kategori (diterangkan, tidak diukur — cth. kumpulan darah) dan numerik (diukur — cth. tinggi), dan data numerik selanjutnya sama ada diskret (kiraan unit bulat) atau selanjar (diukur pada skala).

Once collected and classified, data is organised into a frequency table, then represented visually — bar chart, pie chart, line graph, dot plot, or stem-and-leaf plot — whichever best suits the data and the question being asked. Selepas dikumpul dan dikelaskan, data disusun ke dalam jadual kekerapan, kemudian diwakilkan secara visual — carta palang, carta pai, graf garis, plot titik, atau plot batang-dan-daun — mana-mana yang paling sesuai dengan data dan soalan ditanya.

🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Classify: (a) the body temperature of each student (b) the causes of road accidents. Which is categorical and which is numerical?Kelaskan: (a) suhu badan setiap pelajar (b) punca kemalangan jalan raya. Mana kategori dan mana numerik?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
(a) Numerical — temperature is measured. (b) Categorical — causes are described, not measured. (a) Numerik — suhu diukur. (b) Kategori — punca diterangkan, bukan diukur.
Pie Chart — Angle of Each SectorCarta Pai — Sudut Setiap Sektor
Angle = (Frequency ÷ Total) × 360°

Bar ChartCarta Palang

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A bar chart uses bars of equal width to compare frequencies across categories — best when you want to compare one category directly against another (e.g. "which activity is most popular?"). Bars can run vertically or horizontally, but must be evenly spaced with a consistent scale. Carta palang menggunakan palang lebar sama untuk membanding kekerapan merentasi kategori — terbaik apabila anda mahu banding satu kategori terus dengan satu lagi (cth. "aktiviti manakah paling popular?"). Palang boleh berjalan menegak atau mendatar, tetapi mesti berjarak sama dengan skala konsisten.

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Leisure activities of Form 1 Bakti students: Reading 8, TV 9, Internet 7, Exercise 6, Music 4. Construct a bar chart.Aktiviti riadah pelajar Tingkatan 1 Bakti: Membaca 8, TV 9, Internet 7, Bersenam 6, Muzik 4. Bina carta palang.
📈

Line GraphGraf Garis

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A line graph plots points and connects them with straight lines — best for showing how a value CHANGES OVER TIME (e.g. temperature hour by hour). The direction of the line — rising, falling, or flat — tells the story of the trend at a glance. Graf garis memplot titik dan menyambungkannya dengan garis lurus — terbaik untuk menunjukkan bagaimana nilai BERUBAH DARI SEMASA KE SEMASA (cth. suhu jam demi jam). Arah garis — naik, turun, atau mendatar — menceritakan trend serta-merta.

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
A patient's temperature (°C) over 10 hours: 37.8, 37.9, 38.2, 38.4, 38.2, 37.9, 37.9, 37.6, 37.6, 37.5. Construct a line graph.Suhu pesakit (°C) sepanjang 10 jam: 37.8, 37.9, 38.2, 38.4, 38.2, 37.9, 37.9, 37.6, 37.6, 37.5. Bina graf garis.
⚫

Dot PlotPlot Titik

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A dot plot places one dot above each data value on a number line — stacking dots when a value repeats. It's the fastest way to spot where data CLUSTERS, and to notice any unusual values (outliers) sitting apart from the rest. Plot titik meletakkan satu titik di atas setiap nilai data pada garis nombor — menyusun titik apabila nilai berulang. Ia cara terpantas untuk kesan di mana data BERKELOMPOK, dan perasan sebarang nilai luar biasa (nilai terpencil) berasingan daripada yang lain.

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
The lifespans (nearest hour) of 12 dry cells tested: 10, 11, 11, 12, 12, 12, 13, 13, 13, 13, 14, 15. Construct a dot plot.Jangka hayat (jam terdekat) 12 sel kering diuji: 10, 11, 11, 12, 12, 12, 13, 13, 13, 13, 14, 15. Bina plot titik.
🌿

Stem-and-Leaf PlotPlot Batang-dan-Daun

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A stem-and-leaf plot splits each number into a "stem" (the leading digit(s)) and a "leaf" (the last digit). Unlike a bar chart or dot plot, it keeps every EXACT original value — so you can still do arithmetic on the data afterward, not just see its shape. Plot batang-dan-daun memecahkan setiap nombor kepada "batang" (digit hadapan) dan "daun" (digit terakhir). Berbeza dengan carta palang atau plot titik, ia mengekalkan setiap nilai asal TEPAT — jadi anda masih boleh buat pengiraan pada data selepas itu, bukan sekadar lihat bentuknya.

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Mathematics marks for 20 students: 60,56,69,32,63,58,71,86,52,64,50,67,82,63,75,50,69,78,77,59. Construct a stem-and-leaf plot.Markah Matematik 20 pelajar: 60,56,69,32,63,58,71,86,52,64,50,67,82,63,75,50,69,78,77,59. Bina plot batang-dan-daun.
🔍

Interpreting Data RepresentationsMentafsir Perwakilan Data

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Once a chart is built, the real work is READING it — pulling out specific values, spotting trends, making inferences, and predicting what comes next. Selepas carta dibina, kerja sebenar ialah MEMBACANYA — mengeluarkan nilai khusus, mengesan trend, membuat inferens, dan meramalkan apa yang berlaku seterusnya.

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
The line graph shows waste (thousand tonnes) produced in a city, 2010-2015: 1.9, 2.1, 2.9, 2.6, 2.1, 1.6. (a) Mass in 2010? (b) Compare 2011 and 2014. (c) Find the mean. (d) State one inference. (e) Predict 2016.Graf garis tunjuk sisa (ribu tan) dihasilkan di sebuah bandar, 2010-2015: 1.9, 2.1, 2.9, 2.6, 2.1, 1.6. (a) Jisim 2010? (b) Banding 2011 dan 2014. (c) Cari min. (d) Nyatakan satu inferens. (e) Ramalkan 2016.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Using a pie chart for data with MANY categories, or a stem-and-leaf plot for categorical (non-numeric) data.Guna carta pai untuk data dengan BANYAK kategori, atau plot batang-dan-daun untuk data kategori (bukan numerik).
✅Pie charts suit few categories comparing to a whole; stem-and-leaf plots need numerical data with a clear tens/units split.Carta pai sesuai untuk kategori sedikit dibanding keseluruhan; plot batang-dan-daun perlukan data numerik dengan pembahagian puluh/unit jelas.

Challenge MissionMisi Cabaran

🏆 KBAT

A dot plot shows the lifespans (nearest hour) of 24 dry cells tested in a quality control lab. Explain what additional information a stem-and-leaf plot of the same data could reveal that the dot plot cannot. Plot titik tunjuk jangka hayat (jam terdekat) 24 sel kering diuji di makmal kawalan kualiti. Jelaskan maklumat tambahan apa plot batang-dan-daun data sama boleh dedahkan yang plot titik tidak boleh.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
A stem-and-leaf plot retains the EXACT original data values (via stem and leaf digits), so you can do precise arithmetic on it — a dot plot only shows relative position and clustering, not exact recoverable values. Plot batang-dan-daun mengekalkan nilai data asal TEPAT (melalui digit batang dan daun), jadi anda boleh buat pengiraan tepat — plot titik hanya tunjuk kedudukan relatif dan pengelompokan, bukan nilai tepat boleh dipulihkan.
