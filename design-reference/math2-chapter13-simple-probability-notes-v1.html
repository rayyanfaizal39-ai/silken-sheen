Simple ProbabilityKebarangkalian Mudah

Flip a coin 25 times and heads won't land exactly 12.5 times — but flip it 1,200 times, and the fraction of heads creeps closer and closer to one steady number: 0.5. That steady number is probability. Balingkan syiling 25 kali dan kepala tidak akan mendarat tepat 12.5 kali — tetapi balingkan 1,200 kali, dan pecahan kepala semakin dekat kepada satu nombor tetap: 0.5. Nombor tetap itu ialah kebarangkalian.

13.1

Experimental ProbabilityKebarangkalian Eksperimen

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Every probability sits somewhere between 0 (won't happen) and 1 (will definitely happen). Experimental probability comes from actually doing the trials and counting: how often something happened, out of how many tries. Setiap kebarangkalian terletak antara 0 (tidak akan berlaku) dan 1 (pasti akan berlaku). Kebarangkalian eksperimen datang daripada sebenarnya buat percubaan dan mengira: berapa kerap sesuatu berlaku, daripada berapa kali cubaan.

Experimental ProbabilityKebarangkalian Eksperimen
Experimental P = Frequency of Event ÷ Number of Trials
The MORE trials you run, the closer this gets to the true theoretical probabilitySEMAKIN BANYAK percubaan anda jalankan, semakin dekat ini kepada kebarangkalian teori sebenar
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
A coin is flipped 50 times. Heads appear 23 times. Find the experimental probability of heads.Syiling dibaling 50 kali. Kepala muncul 23 kali. Cari kebarangkalian eksperimen kepala.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

A dice is thrown 16 times. An even number appears 7 times. Write the experimental probability as a ratio.Dadu dibaling 16 kali. Nombor genap muncul 7 kali. Tulis kebarangkalian eksperimen sebagai nisbah.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
7/16 — the frequency of even numbers out of the 16 trials. 7/16 — kekerapan nombor genap daripada 16 percubaan.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Assuming a small number of trials gives a reliable probability.Menganggap bilangan kecil percubaan beri kebarangkalian boleh dipercayai.
✅With only a few trials, results bounce around a lot. Only with MANY trials does the experimental probability settle close to the true value.Dengan hanya beberapa percubaan, keputusan melantun banyak. Hanya dengan BANYAK percubaan kebarangkalian eksperimen mendekati nilai sebenar.
✏️ Uji DiriUji Diri

A spinner is spun 20 times, landing on red 8 times. Find the experimental probability of red.Pemintal diputar 20 kali, mendarat merah 8 kali. Cari kebarangkalian eksperimen merah.
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
8/20 = 2/5
Out of 1200 coin flips, heads appeared 612 times. Is this close to the theoretical 0.5? Explain.Daripada 1200 balingan syiling, kepala muncul 612 kali. Adakah ini dekat dengan teori 0.5? Jelaskan.
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
612/1200 = 0.51 — very close to 0.5, as expected with a large number of trials.612/1200 = 0.51 — sangat dekat dengan 0.5, seperti dijangka dengan bilangan besar percubaan.
A basketball player makes 34 out of 50 free throws in practice. Based on this, estimate how many she'd make out of 200 attempts.Pemain bola keranjang jaringkan 34 daripada 50 lontaran percuma latihan. Berdasarkan ini, anggarkan berapa dia akan jaringkan daripada 200 percubaan.
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Experimental P = 34/50 = 0.68. Estimate = 0.68 × 200 = 136 successful throws.P eksperimen = 34/50 = 0.68. Anggaran = 0.68 × 200 = 136 lontaran berjaya.

13.2

The Probability Theory Involving Equally Likely OutcomesTeori Kebarangkalian Melibatkan Kesudahan Sama Boleh Jadi

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

When every outcome is equally likely (like each face of a fair dice), you don't need an experiment at all — just COUNT. Theoretical probability is the number of ways your event can happen, out of the total number of equally-likely outcomes. Apabila setiap kesudahan sama mungkin (seperti setiap muka dadu adil), anda tidak perlukan eksperimen langsung — cuma KIRA. Kebarangkalian teori ialah bilangan cara peristiwa anda boleh berlaku, daripada jumlah kesudahan sama mungkin.

Theoretical ProbabilityKebarangkalian Teori
P(A) = n(A) ÷ n(S)
n(A) = ways the event can happen, n(S) = total possible outcomesn(A) = cara peristiwa boleh berlaku, n(S) = jumlah kesudahan mungkin
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Two fair dice are thrown together. Find the probability their sum equals 5.Dua dadu adil dibaling bersama. Cari kebarangkalian jumlahnya bersamaan 5.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

A box has 25 green apples and 35 red apples. Find the probability of picking a green apple.Kotak ada 25 epal hijau dan 35 epal merah. Cari kebarangkalian memilih epal hijau.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
P(green) = 25/60 = 5/12.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Dividing by the number of possible SUMS (like 2 through 12) instead of the total number of dice combinations.Bahagi dengan bilangan JUMLAH mungkin (seperti 2 hingga 12) bukan jumlah gabungan dadu.
✅With two dice, n(S) is always 36 (6×6 combinations) — count how many of THOSE 36 give your target sum.Dengan dua dadu, n(S) sentiasa 36 (gabungan 6×6) — kira berapa daripada 36 itu beri jumlah sasaran anda.
✏️ Uji DiriUji Diri
EasyMudah
MediumSederhana
HardSukar
A fair dice is thrown once. Find P(getting number 4).Dadu adil dibaling sekali. Cari P(dapat nombor 4).
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
1/6
Pramjit gets RM5 pocket money every Tue, Wed, Thu. Find P(getting RM5) on a random day across 4 weeks.Pramjit dapat RM5 wang saku setiap Sel, Rab, Kha. Cari P(dapat RM5) pada hari rawak sepanjang 4 minggu.
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
n(A)=12 (3 days×4 weeks), n(S)=28. P=12/28=3/7.
Two fair dice are thrown. Find the probability their sum is greater than 9.Dua dadu adil dibaling. Cari kebarangkalian jumlahnya lebih daripada 9.
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Sums >9: 10,10,10,11,11,12 (6 combos: (4,6)(5,5)(6,4)(5,6)(6,5)(6,6)). P=6/36=1/6.Jumlah >9: (4,6)(5,5)(6,4)(5,6)(6,5)(6,6) — 6 gabungan. P=6/36=1/6.

13.3

Complement of An Event ProbabilityKebarangkalian Pelengkap Peristiwa

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

The complement of an event is simply "everything else that could happen instead." Since SOMETHING must happen, an event and its complement always add up to exactly 1. Pelengkap peristiwa hanyalah "segala-galanya lain yang boleh berlaku sebaliknya." Oleh kerana SESUATU mesti berlaku, peristiwa dan pelengkapnya sentiasa berjumlah tepat 1.

Complement RulePeraturan Pelengkap
P(A) + P(A') = 1
So P(A') = 1 − P(A) — no need to count the complement directly if you already know P(A)Jadi P(A') = 1 − P(A) — tidak perlu kira pelengkap terus jika anda sudah tahu P(A)
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
A number is chosen from 1 to 20. A is the event of choosing a prime number. Find P(A').Nombor dipilih dari 1 hingga 20. A ialah peristiwa memilih nombor perdana. Cari P(A').
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

A box has red and blue pens. P(choosing blue) = 3/5. Find P(choosing red).Kotak ada pen merah dan biru. P(pilih biru) = 3/5. Cari P(pilih merah).

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
P(red) = 1 − 3/5 = 2/5.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Recounting the complement's elements from scratch when P(A) is already known.Mengira semula unsur pelengkap dari awal apabila P(A) sudah diketahui.
✅If you already have P(A), just subtract from 1 — it's faster and avoids re-listing everything.Jika anda sudah ada P(A), hanya tolak daripada 1 — lebih pantas dan elak menyenaraikan semula semuanya.
✏️ Uji DiriUji Diri

MediumSederhana

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan

A container has 5 red, 8 sambal, 4 chocolate buns. A is getting a chocolate bun. Describe A' in words.Bekas ada 5 bun merah, 8 sambal, 4 coklat. A ialah dapat bun coklat. Terangkan A' dalam perkataan.
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
A' is getting a bun that is NOT chocolate (red or sambal).A' ialah dapat bun yang BUKAN coklat (merah atau sambal).
10% of oranges from a box of 30 are rotten. C is a non-rotten orange event. Find P(C).10% oren daripada kotak 30 reput. C ialah peristiwa oren tidak reput. Cari P(C).
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
P(rotten)=0.1, so P(C)=1−0.1=0.9.

13.4

Simple ProbabilityKebarangkalian Mudah

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Real problems dress probability up in a story — a lucky draw, a shopping trip, a bus schedule. Strip the story away to find n(A) and n(S), and the rest is the same formula every time. Masalah sebenar hiaskan kebarangkalian dalam cerita — cabutan bertuah, perjalanan membeli-belah, jadual bas. Buang cerita untuk cari n(A) dan n(S), dan selebihnya formula sama setiap kali.

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
A supermarket gives one lucky-draw entry per RM50 spent, distributing 30 entries a day for a week (210 total). Danial spends RM450. Find his probability of winning.Pasar raya beri satu penyertaan cabutan bertuah setiap RM50 dibelanja, mengedar 30 penyertaan sehari selama seminggu (210 jumlah). Danial belanja RM450. Cari kebarangkalian dia menang.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

A bicycle shop has 35 bicycles and sold 15 in January. Find the probability that a randomly picked bicycle was sold in January.Kedai basikal ada 35 basikal dan jual 15 pada Januari. Cari kebarangkalian basikal dipilih rawak dijual pada Januari.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
P = 15/35 = 3/7.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Getting lost in the story details before identifying what n(A) and n(S) actually are.Hilang dalam butiran cerita sebelum kenal pasti apa n(A) dan n(S) sebenarnya.
✅Underline the total possible outcomes first (n(S)), then the favourable ones (n(A)) — everything else is just context.Garis bawah jumlah kesudahan mungkin dahulu (n(S)), kemudian yang memihak (n(A)) — selainnya cuma konteks.
✏️ Uji DiriUji Diri
EasyMudah
MediumSederhana
HardSukar
A class has 20 students, 12 of whom play football. Find P(a randomly picked student plays football).Kelas ada 20 pelajar, 12 bermain bola sepak. Cari P(pelajar dipilih rawak bermain bola sepak).
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
12/20 = 3/5
The Meteorological Department predicts rain every 3 days in a 30-day month. Find the probability of rain on a random day.Jabatan Meteorologi ramal hujan setiap 3 hari dalam bulan 30 hari. Cari kebarangkalian hujan pada hari rawak.
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
n(A)=30÷3=10 rain days. P=10/30=1/3.
Ali has RM73 to buy shoes: 3 pairs under RM50, 4 pairs RM50-70, 5 pairs at RM70+. If B is the event Ali buys a pair he can afford, express B' in words.Ali ada RM73 untuk beli kasut: 3 pasang bawah RM50, 4 pasang RM50-70, 5 pasang RM70+. Jika B ialah peristiwa Ali beli pasangan mampu dibeli, nyatakan B' dalam perkataan.
👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
B' is Ali buying a pair he CANNOT afford with RM73 — the pairs priced at RM70 or more that would still leave him short after other costs, or simply outside his choice set.B' ialah Ali beli pasangan yang TIDAK MAMPU dengan RM73 — pasangan berharga RM70 atau lebih.

A bag has 4 red, 3 blue, and 5 green marbles. One marble is drawn at random. Find (a) P(red), (b) P(NOT green) using the complement rule. Beg ada 4 guli merah, 3 biru, dan 5 hijau. Satu guli diambil rawak. Cari (a) P(merah), (b) P(BUKAN hijau) guna peraturan pelengkap.
