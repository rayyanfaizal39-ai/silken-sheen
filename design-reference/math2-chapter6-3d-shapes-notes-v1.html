Three-Dimensional Geometrical ShapesBentuk Geometri Tiga Matra

Unfold a box flat and you get a 2D pattern called a net. Fold it back up, and it's 3D again. Once you can see shapes both ways — flat and folded — surface area and volume both become much easier. Buka kotak rata dan anda dapat corak 2D dipanggil bentangan. Lipat semula, dan ia 3D semula. Sebaik anda boleh lihat bentuk kedua-dua cara — rata dan dilipat — luas permukaan dan isipadu kedua-duanya menjadi lebih mudah.

6.1

Geometric Properties of Three-Dimensional ShapesSifat Geometri Bentuk Tiga Matra

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Every 3D shape here is built from flat 2D shapes. What tells them apart is their BASE shape and whether their sides are flat or curved. Setiap bentuk 3D di sini dibina daripada bentuk 2D rata. Apa yang bezakannya ialah bentuk TAPAK dan sama ada sisinya rata atau melengkung.

Prism vs PyramidPrisma lwn Piramid
Prism: 2 identical bases. Pyramid: 1 base + a point (apex).Prisma: 2 tapak serupa. Piramid: 1 tapak + hujung (apeks).
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Name the shape: 2 circular bases, 1 curved surface, no apex.Namakan bentuk: 2 tapak bulat, 1 permukaan melengkung, tiada apeks.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Is a cube a prism? Explain.Adakah kubus prisma? Jelaskan.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Yes — a cube has 2 identical square bases (top and bottom) with a uniform cross-section — that fits the prism definition. Ya — kubus ada 2 tapak segi empat sama serupa (atas dan bawah) dengan keratan rentas seragam — itu memenuhi definisi prisma.

✅Curved surface = cone or cylinder or sphere. Flat triangular sides meeting at a point = pyramid.Permukaan melengkung = kon atau silinder atau sfera. Sisi segi tiga rata bertemu di satu titik = piramid.

6.2

Nets of Three-Dimensional ShapesBentangan Bentuk Tiga Matra

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A net is what you get when you unfold a 3D shape flat, so every face lies open. Fold it back along the lines, and it becomes 3D again. Bentangan ialah apa yang anda dapat bila membuka bentuk 3D rata, supaya setiap muka terbuka. Lipat semula sepanjang garis, dan ia menjadi 3D semula.

The same shape can have MORE than one valid netBentuk sama boleh ada LEBIH daripada satu bentangan sah
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
A cylinder's net is made of which shapes?Bentangan silinder dibina daripada bentuk apa?
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

What shapes make up the net of a cone?Bentuk apa membina bentangan kon?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
One circle (the base) plus one curved sector (the side that wraps around to a point). Satu bulatan (tapak) tambah satu sektor melengkung (sisi yang membalut ke satu titik).
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Assuming a cube has only ONE possible net.Menganggap kubus hanya ada SATU bentangan mungkin.
✅A cube has 11 different valid nets — as long as all 6 squares connect and fold up correctly, it works.Kubus ada 11 bentangan sah berbeza — asalkan kesemua 6 segi empat sama bersambung dan lipat dengan betul, ia berfungsi.

6.3

Surface Area of Three-Dimensional ShapesLuas Permukaan Bentuk Tiga Matra

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Since the net IS the shape laid flat, its surface area is just the total area of every flat face in that net, added together. Oleh kerana bentangan IALAH bentuk dibaringkan rata, luas permukaannya hanyalah jumlah luas setiap muka rata dalam bentangan itu, ditambah bersama.

CuboidKuboid
Surface area = sum of all 6 rectangle facesLuas permukaan = jumlah semua 6 muka segi empat tepat
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Find the surface area of a cuboid 5 cm × 4 cm × 3 cm.Cari luas permukaan kuboid 5 cm × 4 cm × 3 cm.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

A closed cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Its net is 2 circles + 1 rectangle (width = circumference). Find its surface area (π=22/7).Silinder tertutup ada jejari 7 cm dan tinggi 10 cm. Bentangannya 2 bulatan + 1 segi empat tepat (lebar = lilitan). Cari luas permukaan (π=22/7).

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
2 circles = 2×22/7×49=308. Rectangle = circumference×height = (2×22/7×7)×10=440. Total = 748 cm². 2 bulatan = 2×22/7×49=308. Segi empat tepat = lilitan×tinggi = (2×22/7×7)×10=440. Jumlah = 748 cm².
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Forgetting to double the base area for prisms and cylinders (there are TWO identical bases).Terlupa gandakan luas tapak untuk prisma dan silinder (ada DUA tapak serupa).
✅Picture the net — if it has 2 base faces, count both. If it has 1 (like a pyramid or cone), count only 1.Bayangkan bentangan — jika ada 2 muka tapak, kira kedua-dua. Jika ada 1 (seperti piramid atau kon), kira 1 sahaja.

6.4

Volume of Three-Dimensional ShapesIsipadu Bentuk Tiga Matra

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Stack identical coin-shaped discs on top of each other and you build a cylinder. For ANY prism or cylinder, volume is just the base area, stacked up to the full height. Susun cakera berbentuk syiling serupa di atas satu sama lain dan anda bina silinder. Untuk SEBARANG prisma atau silinder, isipadu hanyalah luas tapak, disusun sehingga ketinggian penuh.

Prisms and CylindersPrisma dan Silinder
Volume = Base Area × HeightIsipadu = Luas Tapak × Tinggi
📖 Pyramids and Cones — Exactly ⅓Piramid dan Kon — Tepat ⅓

Fill a cone with sand, then pour it into a same-size cylinder. It takes exactly THREE cones to fill that cylinder — so a cone always holds exactly ⅓ of the matching cylinder's volume. The same ⅓ relationship holds between a pyramid and a prism sharing the same base and height. Isi kon dengan pasir, kemudian tuang ke silinder saiz sama. Ia mengambil tepat TIGA kon untuk isi silinder itu — jadi kon sentiasa mengandungi tepat ⅓ isipadu silinder sepadan. Perhubungan ⅓ sama berlaku antara piramid dan prisma berkongsi tapak dan tinggi sama.

Pyramids and ConesPiramid dan Kon
Volume = ⅓ × Base Area × HeightIsipadu = ⅓ × Luas Tapak × Tinggi
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Find the volume of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 20 cm (π=22/7).Cari isipadu silinder berjejari 7 cm dan tinggi 20 cm (π=22/7).
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

A square-based pyramid has base 6cm×6cm and height 10cm. Find its volume.Piramid tapak segi empat sama 6cm×6cm dan tinggi 10cm. Cari isipadunya.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
⅓ × 36 × 10 = 120 cm³.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Forgetting the ⅓ for pyramids and cones — treating them like prisms.Terlupa ⅓ untuk piramid dan kon — melayan seperti prisma.
✅A shape coming to a POINT (apex) always needs the ⅓. A shape with two identical flat ends does not.Bentuk yang meruncing ke SATU TITIK (apeks) sentiasa perlukan ⅓. Bentuk dengan dua hujung rata serupa tidak.
