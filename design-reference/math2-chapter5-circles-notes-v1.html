CirclesBulatan

A clock's second hand sweeps a perfect circle every 60 seconds. Every point it passes sits exactly the same distance from the centre — that one idea is the seed of everything in this chapter. Jarum saat jam mengimbas bulatan sempurna setiap 60 saat. Setiap titik yang dilaluinya terletak tepat jarak sama dari pusat — satu idea itu benih kepada segalanya dalam bab ini.

5.1

Properties of CirclesSifat Bulatan

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A circle is every point sitting the SAME distance from one fixed centre. Once you know the parts' names, every circle rule in this chapter becomes easy to talk about. Bulatan ialah setiap titik terletak jarak SAMA dari satu pusat tetap. Sebaik anda tahu nama bahagiannya, setiap peraturan bulatan dalam bab ini mudah dibincangkan.

Key PartsBahagian Utama
Diameter = 2 × RadiusDiameter = 2 × Jejari
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Name the parts labeled in the diagram above: the line from centre to edge, the line all the way across through the centre, and the curve joining two points on the circumference.Namakan bahagian dalam gambar rajah di atas: garis dari pusat ke tepi, garis merentasi terus melalui pusat, dan lengkung menghubungkan dua titik pada lilitan.

✅Every diameter IS a chord, but only the one passing through the CENTRE — other chords don't.Setiap diameter IALAH perentas, tetapi hanya yang melalui PUSAT — perentas lain tidak.

5.2

Symmetry and ChordsSimetri dan Perentas

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Fold a circle exactly in half along any diameter, and the two halves match perfectly — that's why every diameter is a line of symmetry. Draw a radius that meets a chord at a right angle, and it splits that chord into two EQUAL halves. Lipat bulatan tepat separuh di sepanjang sebarang diameter, dan dua separuh sepadan sempurna — itu sebab setiap diameter garis simetri. Lukis jejari bertemu perentas pada sudut tegak, dan ia membahagi perentas itu kepada dua separuh SAMA.

Perpendicular Radius Bisects the ChordJejari Serenjang Membahagi Dua Perentas
Right angle at the chord → equal halvesSudut tegak di perentas → separuh sama
The radius, half-chord, and full radius form a right-angled triangle — Pythagoras applies!Jejari, separuh-perentas, dan jejari penuh bentuk segi tiga bersudut tegak — Pythagoras terpakai!
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
A radius OP is perpendicular to chord MN. If the radius is 10 cm and OS (the distance from centre to chord) is 8 cm, find the length of MN.Jejari OP serenjang dengan perentas MN. Jika jejari 10 cm dan OS (jarak pusat ke perentas) 8 cm, cari panjang MN.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Two equal chords RS and TU exist in a circle. Are their arcs the same length?Dua perentas sama RS dan TU wujud dalam bulatan. Adakah lengkoknya panjang sama?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Yes — equal chords always produce arcs of equal length, and are equally distant from the centre. Ya — perentas sama sentiasa menghasilkan lengkok panjang sama, dan sama jarak dari pusat.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Assuming ANY radius bisects a chord it touches.Menganggap SEBARANG jejari membahagi dua perentas yang disentuhnya.
✅Only a radius meeting the chord at exactly 90° bisects it — check for the right angle first.Hanya jejari bertemu perentas pada tepat 90° membahagi duanya — semak sudut tegak dahulu.

5.3

Circumference and Area of a CircleLilitan dan Luas Bulatan

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Slice a circle into thin pizza-like sectors and lay them side by side — they roughly form a rectangle. The more slices, the straighter the edges get. That rectangle's height is the radius, and its base is HALF the circumference. Hiris bulatan kepada sektor nipis seperti piza dan susun bersebelahan — ia lebih kurang membentuk segi empat tepat. Semakin banyak hirisan, semakin lurus tepinya. Tinggi segi empat tepat itu jejari, dan tapaknya SEPARUH lilitan.

Circumference and AreaLilitan dan Luas
C = 2πr   |   A = πr²
Area = base × height of that "unrolled" rectangle = (πr) × r = πr²Luas = tapak × tinggi segi empat tepat "digulung" itu = (πr) × r = πr²
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Calculate the circumference of a circle with diameter 14 cm (use π=22/7).Kira lilitan bulatan berdiameter 14 cm (guna π=22/7).
Length of an ArcPanjang Lengkok
Arc length = (θ/360°) × 2πr
An arc is just a FRACTION of the whole circumference — the fraction is θ out of a full 360°Lengkok hanyalah PECAHAN keseluruhan lilitan — pecahan itu θ daripada 360° penuh
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Find the length of a minor arc that spans 60° in a circle of radius 14 cm.Cari panjang lengkok minor merangkumi 60° dalam bulatan berjejari 14 cm.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Find the area of a circle with radius 21 cm (use π=22/7).Cari luas bulatan berjejari 21 cm (guna π=22/7).

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
A = 22/7 × 21² = 22/7 × 441 = 1386 cm².
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Using the diameter instead of radius in A = πr².Guna diameter bukan jejari dalam A = πr².
✅Area ALWAYS needs the radius — if you're given the diameter, halve it first.Luas SENTIASA perlukan jejari — jika diberi diameter, separuhkan dahulu.
