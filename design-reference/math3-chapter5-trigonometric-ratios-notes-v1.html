Trigonometric RatiosNisbah Trigonometri

You can't climb a mountain to measure its height — but stand at a known distance, measure the angle up to the peak, and trigonometry hands you the height without ever leaving the ground. Anda tidak boleh panjat gunung untuk ukur ketinggiannya — tetapi berdiri pada jarak diketahui, ukur sudut ke puncak, dan trigonometri beri anda ketinggian tanpa tinggalkan tanah.

5.1

Sine, Cosine and Tangent of Acute AnglesSinus, Kosinus dan Tangen Sudut Tirus

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

The hypotenuse is always the longest side, opposite the right angle — that one never changes. But which side is opposite and which is adjacent depends on WHICH acute angle you're looking from. Hipotenus sentiasa sisi terpanjang, bertentangan sudut tegak — itu tidak pernah berubah. Tetapi sisi mana bertentangan dan mana bersebelahan bergantung pada sudut tirus MANA anda melihat.

SOH-CAH-TOASOH-CAH-TOA
sin θ = opp/hyp  |  cos θ = adj/hyp  |  tan θ = opp/adj
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
A right triangle has opposite=3, adjacent=4, hypotenuse=5 (relative to angle x). Find sin x, cos x, tan x.Segi tiga tegak ada bertentangan=3, bersebelahan=4, hipotenus=5 (relatif sudut x). Cari sin x, cos x, tan x.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

If sin x = ⅗, what must cos x and tan x be, using the same 3-4-5 triangle?Jika sin x = ⅗, apakah cos x dan tan x, guna segi tiga 3-4-5 sama?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
cos x = 4/5, tan x = 3/4 — the same triangle gives all three ratios at once. cos x = 4/5, tan x = 3/4 — segi tiga sama beri kesemua tiga nisbah serentak.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Labeling opposite/adjacent based on the triangle's position instead of the marked angle.Menamakan bertentangan/bersebelahan berdasarkan kedudukan segi tiga bukan sudut ditanda.
✅Always start from the marked angle. The side touching it (not the hypotenuse) is adjacent; the side across from it is opposite.Sentiasa mula daripada sudut ditanda. Sisi menyentuhnya (bukan hipotenus) ialah bersebelahan; sisi merentasinya ialah bertentangan.

Why?Segi tiga A bertentangan=9, bersebelahan=12, hip=15 (sudut x). Segi tiga B bertentangan=3, bersebelahan=4, hip=5 (sudut y). Adakah sin x dan sin y sama? Kenapa?

5.1

Finding Missing Sides and AnglesMencari Sisi dan Sudut Hilang

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Know an angle and ONE side? SOH-CAH-TOA finds any other side. Pick whichever ratio connects the side you KNOW to the side you WANT. Tahu sudut dan SATU sisi? SOH-CAH-TOA cari sebarang sisi lain. Pilih nisbah yang menghubungkan sisi anda TAHU dengan sisi anda MAHU.

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Right triangle PQR: ∠QPR=90°, QR=15, PQ=8. Find (a) PR, (b) sin∠PRQ, (c) tan∠QPR.Segi tiga tegak PQR: ∠QPR=90°, QR=15, PQ=8. Cari (a) PR, (b) sin∠PRQ, (c) tan∠QPR.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

A ladder leans against a wall, making a 60° angle with the ground. If the ladder is 4m long, how high up the wall does it reach? (height = hyp × sin60°)Tangga bersandar dinding, buat sudut 60° dengan tanah. Jika tangga 4m panjang, berapa tinggi ia sampai dinding? (tinggi = hip × sin60°)

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
height = 4 × sin60° = 4 × 0.866 ≈ 3.46 m. tinggi = 4 × sin60° = 4 × 0.866 ≈ 3.46 m.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Picking a ratio that doesn't involve the side you actually know.Memilih nisbah tidak melibatkan sisi anda sebenarnya tahu.
✅List which sides you know and want FIRST, then choose whichever of sin/cos/tan connects exactly those two.Senaraikan sisi anda tahu dan mahu DAHULU, kemudian pilih sin/cos/tan yang hubungkan tepat kedua-dua itu.
