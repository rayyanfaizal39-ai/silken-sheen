💥 Science Blog — Supernova Explosions

A supernova — the explosive death of a massive star — releases 100 times more light energy than our entire Sun. The last one visible to the naked eye was detected in 1987, even though the explosion itself happened around 400 years earlier — the light just took that long to reach us.

11.1

Galaxies in the Universe

What is a galaxy?

A galaxy is a massive collection of millions (often billions) of stars, held together with gas and dust by gravity. There are millions of galaxies in the universe, and they come in three main shapes.

Our address in the universe

Our solar system sits near the edge of one of the spiral arms of the Milky Way — a medium-large spiral galaxy home to roughly 200 billion stars, and the Sun is just one of them.

🌌 The Milky Way▾

✨ A cluster of galaxies▾

Groups of galaxies bound together by gravity — the Milky Way is part of one such group.

♾️ The Universe▾

Everything that exists — millions of galaxy clusters, with no observed end.

What determines whether a dying star becomes a white dwarf or a black hole?Reveal hint
