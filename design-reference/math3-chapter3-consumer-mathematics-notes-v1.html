Consumer Mathematics: Savings and Investments, Credit and DebtMatematik Pengguna: Simpanan dan Pelaburan, Kredit dan Hutang

Two people save the same amount at the same rate — but one earns interest on their interest, and after a few years that difference becomes real money. This chapter is about the math behind your future bank balance. Dua orang simpan jumlah sama pada kadar sama — tetapi satu perolehi faedah atas faedahnya, dan selepas beberapa tahun perbezaan itu menjadi wang sebenar. Bab ini tentang matematik di sebalik baki bank masa depan anda.

3.1

Savings and InvestmentsSimpanan dan Pelaburan

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Simple interest pays you the same amount every year, based only on your original deposit. Compound interest pays you interest on your ORIGINAL deposit PLUS every year's earned interest — so the amount you earn keeps growing. Faedah mudah bayar anda jumlah sama setiap tahun, berdasarkan deposit asal sahaja. Faedah kompaun bayar anda faedah atas deposit ASAL TAMBAH faedah diperoleh setiap tahun — jadi jumlah anda perolehi terus berkembang.

Simple InterestFaedah Mudah
I = Prt
P= principal, r= rate (as a decimal), t= time in years
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Encik Badrul deposits RM500 at 3% per annum for 2 years. Find the total interest earned.Encik Badrul deposit RM500 pada 3% setahun selama 2 tahun. Cari jumlah faedah diperoleh.
Compound InterestFaedah Kompaun
MV = P(1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ
MV= matured value, n= times compounded per year
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
RM800 is deposited at 3% per annum, compounded yearly. Find the savings after 2 years.RM800 dideposit pada 3% setahun, dikompaun tahunan. Cari simpanan selepas 2 tahun.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

RM1500 is saved at 4% per annum, compounded every 6 months (n=2). Find the total savings after 1 year.RM1500 disimpan pada 4% setahun, dikompaun setiap 6 bulan (n=2). Cari jumlah simpanan selepas 1 tahun.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
MV = 1500(1+0.04/2)^(2×1) = 1500(1.02)² = RM1560.60.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Forgetting to divide the rate by n, or multiply the time by n, when interest compounds more than once a year.Terlupa bahagi kadar dengan n, atau darab masa dengan n, apabila faedah kompaun lebih daripada sekali setahun.
✅Both r/n AND the exponent nt need the SAME n — if compounding twice a year, both change together.Kedua-dua r/n DAN eksponen nt perlukan n SAMA — jika kompaun dua kali setahun, kedua-dua berubah bersama.

3.2

Credit and Debt ManagementPengurusan Kredit dan Hutang

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Credit is permission to pay LATER — a bank pays the seller now, and you repay the bank within an agreed period. Whatever you haven't paid back yet becomes your debt. Kredit ialah kebenaran untuk bayar KEMUDIAN — bank bayar penjual sekarang, dan anda bayar semula bank dalam tempoh dipersetujui. Apa sahaja belum dibayar semula menjadi hutang anda.

Managing Credit WiselyMengurus Kredit dengan Bijak
Pay in full within the interest-free period = no extra chargesBayar penuh dalam tempoh bebas faedah = tiada caj tambahan
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Puan Zuraidah wants to buy a fridge but is short RM2500 in cash. A shop offers an instant loan at 4% per annum, and she also has a credit card. Which should she use, and why?Puan Zuraidah mahu beli peti sejuk tetapi kurang RM2500 tunai. Kedai tawar pinjaman segera 4% setahun, dan dia juga ada kad kredit. Mana dia patut guna, dan kenapa?
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Encik Syed buys an air conditioner and is RM1200 short in cash, but knows he'll have the money by the end of the month when his salary arrives. Which credit facility fits best?Encik Syed beli penyaman udara dan kurang RM1200 tunai, tetapi tahu dia akan ada wang hujung bulan bila gaji tiba. Kemudahan kredit mana paling sesuai?

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
A credit card — if he repays within the interest-free period (before his salary-payday statement), he avoids all extra charges. Kad kredit — jika dia bayar semula dalam tempoh bebas faedah (sebelum penyata hari gaji), dia elak semua caj tambahan.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Paying only the credit card's minimum amount, thinking the debt is under control.Bayar hanya jumlah minimum kad kredit, menyangka hutang terkawal.
✅Paying only the minimum lets the bank charge interest on the REST — the debt keeps growing even while you're "paying" it.Bayar minimum sahaja bank kenakan faedah pada BAKI — hutang terus berkembang walaupun anda "membayarnya".
