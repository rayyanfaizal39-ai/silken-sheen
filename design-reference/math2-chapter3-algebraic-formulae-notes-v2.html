3.1

Forming FormulaeMembentuk Rumus

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

An algebraic formula is a rule connecting variables. The letter standing ALONE on one side is the subject. Rumus algebra ialah peraturan menghubungkan pemboleh ubah. Huruf berdiri SENDIRIAN pada satu belah ialah perkara.

Subject of the FormulaPerkara Rumus
P = a + 2b
Pis the subject — alone on the leftialah perkara — sendirian di kiri
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Suzi sells cake at RM3/slice, cheese cake at double that. A 10% discount applies to everything. Form a formula for the selling price z, for m chocolate slices and n cheese slices.Suzi jual kek RM3/hiris, kek keju dua kali harga itu. Diskaun 10% dikenakan pada semuanya. Bentuk rumus harga jualan z, bagi m hiris kek coklat dan n hiris kek keju.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

A taxi charges RM3 flag-down plus RM2 per km. Write a formula for the fare F for k km.Teksi caj RM3 tambang minimum tambah RM2 setiap km. Tulis rumus tambang F bagi k km.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
F = 3 + 2k
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Applying a discount to only PART of the total.Mengenakan diskaun hanya pada SEBAHAGIAN jumlah.
✅Add up the FULL price first, THEN discount the combined total once.Jumlahkan harga PENUH dahulu, KEMUDIAN diskaun jumlah gabungan sekali.

3.1

Changing the Subject of FormulaMenukar Perkara Rumus

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Think of a formula as a balanced scale. Whatever you do to ONE side, you must do to the OTHER — that keeps it balanced while you move things around to isolate a different letter. Fikirkan rumus sebagai neraca seimbang. Apa sahaja anda buat pada SATU belah, anda mesti buat pada belah SATU LAGI — itu kekalkan imbangan semasa anda alih benda untuk asingkan huruf berbeza.

Worked Example — LinearContoh Berpandu — Linear
State m as the subject: b = 2s − mNyatakan m sebagai perkara: b = 2s − m
Squares and Square Roots Undo Each OtherKuasa Dua dan Punca Saling Batal
s = p²  ⟹  p = √s
Worked Example — Squares/RootsContoh Berpandu — Kuasa Dua/Punca
State p as the subject: t = 1/p²Nyatakan p sebagai perkara: t = 1/p²
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

State p as the subject: w = p/3Nyatakan p sebagai perkara: w = p/3

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Multiply both sides by 3: p = 3w. Darab kedua-dua belah dengan 3: p = 3w.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Leaving the answer as −m instead of finishing to a POSITIVE m.Membiarkan jawapan −m tanpa selesaikan kepada m POSITIF.
✅Ended with −m? Multiply BOTH sides by −1 to flip every sign.Berakhir dengan −m? Darab KEDUA-DUA belah dengan −1 untuk tukar setiap tanda.

3.1

Determining the Value of a VariableMenentukan Nilai Pemboleh Ubah

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Once you have known values, plug them into the formula, then solve for whatever's left. Sebaik anda ada nilai diketahui, masukkan ke dalam rumus, kemudian selesaikan yang tinggal.

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Given m = ¼(p − q)², find q when m = 16 and p = 3.Diberi m = ¼(p − q)², cari q apabila m = 16 dan p = 3.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Given w = 7t − 5u, find t when w = 15 and u = 4.Diberi w = 7t − 5u, cari t apabila w = 15 dan u = 4.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
7t − 20 = 15. 7t = 35. t = 5.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Square-rooting only part of a bracket instead of the whole thing.Cari punca kuasa dua sebahagian kurungan sahaja, bukan keseluruhan.
✅Square-rooting means the ENTIRE bracket gets rooted together.Mencari punca kuasa dua bermaksud KESELURUHAN kurungan dicari bersama.

3.1

Solving ProblemsMenyelesaikan Masalah

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Real problems don't hand you a formula — you build one. Name the unknown with a letter, turn the story into an equation, then solve. Masalah sebenar tidak beri rumus — anda bina satu. Namakan yang tidak diketahui dengan huruf, tukar cerita kepada persamaan, kemudian selesaikan.

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Chicken costs twice a bun. With RM5, Azman buys 2 buns and 1 chicken, RM1 left over. With RM12 buying the same buns, how many chickens can he afford?Ayam harga dua kali bun. Dengan RM5, Azman beli 2 bun dan 1 ayam, baki RM1. Dengan RM12 beli bun sama, berapa ayam mampu dibeli?
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Clothes sell at RMy, discounted to RM(y−8). 5 items cost RM60 total. Find y.Pakaian jual RMy, diskaun RM(y−8). 5 item kos RM60. Cari y.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
5(y−8) = 60. y−8 = 12. y = 20.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Jumping to arithmetic without defining what your letter means.Terus ke pengiraan tanpa tentukan maksud huruf anda.
✅Always write "Let x = ..." first.Sentiasa tulis "Biar x = ..." dahulu.
