3.1

Squares and Square RootsKuasa Dua dan Punca Kuasa Dua

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A square with sides of 4 units has area 4 × 4 = 16 unit². We write 4² = 16 — "the square of 4". Numbers like 1, 4, 9, 16, 25… are perfect squares. Segi empat sama bersisi 4 unit mempunyai luas 4 × 4 = 16 unit². Kita tulis 4² = 16 — "kuasa dua bagi 4". Nombor seperti 1, 4, 9, 16, 25… ialah kuasa dua sempurna.

Squaring and square-rooting are opposite operations. If area = 36 unit², then side length — the square root — is 6, written √36 = 6. Menguasaduakan dan mencari punca kuasa dua adalah operasi bertentangan. Jika luas = 36 unit², maka panjang sisi — punca kuasa dua — ialah 6, ditulis √36 = 6.

Inverse OperationsOperasi Songsang
√(a × a) = a
a² = a × a
√a² = a
Checking a Perfect SquareMenyemak Kuasa Dua Sempurna
Prime-factorise, then group the factors into identical PAIRS.Pemfaktoran perdana, kemudian kumpulkan faktor kepada PASANGAN yang sama.

✅54 = 2×3×3×3 — can't split into two identical groups, so it's NOT a perfect square.54 = 2×3×3×3 — tidak boleh pecah dua kumpulan sama, jadi BUKAN kuasa dua sempurna.

3.2

Cubes and Cube RootsKuasa Tiga dan Punca Kuasa Tiga

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

A cube with sides of 2 units contains 2×2×2 = 8 unit cubes. We write 2³ = 8 — "the cube of 2". Numbers like 1, 8, 27, 64… are perfect cubes. Kubus bersisi 2 unit mengandungi 2×2×2 = 8 unit kubus. Kita tulis 2³ = 8 — "kuasa tiga bagi 2". Nombor seperti 1, 8, 27, 64… ialah kuasa tiga sempurna.

If volume = 8 unit³, side length — the cube root — is 2, written ∛8 = 2. Unlike squares, cubing a negative number always gives a negative result. Jika isi padu = 8 unit³, panjang sisi — punca kuasa tiga — ialah 2, ditulis ∛8 = 2. Berbeza dengan kuasa dua, menguasatigakan nombor negatif sentiasa memberi hasil negatif.

Inverse OperationsOperasi Songsang
∛(a × a × a) = a
a³ = a × a × a
∛a³ = a
Checking a Perfect CubeMenyemak Kuasa Tiga Sempurna
Prime-factorise, then group the factors into identical TRIPLES.Pemfaktoran perdana, kemudian kumpulkan faktor kepada TIGA yang sama.
👁️ Show MethodTunjuk Kaedah
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
A sculptor carves a small cube from a 6 cm wooden block. Remaining wood: 189 cm³. Find the small cube's side length.Pengukir mengukir kubus kecil daripada blok kayu 6 cm. Baki kayu: 189 cm³. Cari panjang sisi kubus kecil.
👁️ Show Answer & MethodTunjuk Jawapan & Kaedah
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Find ∛216 without a calculator.Cari ∛216 tanpa kalkulator.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
216 = 6×6×6 = 6³. So ∛216 = 6. 216 = 6×6×6 = 6³. Jadi ∛216 = 6.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Writing 2³ = 2 × 3 = 6.Menulis 2³ = 2 × 3 = 6.
✅2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 — the base multiplied by ITSELF, 3 times.2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 — asas didarab dengan DIRINYA, 3 kali.
