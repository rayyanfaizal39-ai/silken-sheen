Straight LinesGaris Lurus

y = mx + c packs an entire straight line into one short equation — m tells you the slope, c tells you where it crosses the y-axis. Once you can read that equation, you can predict exactly where any line goes, and exactly where two lines will meet. y = mx + c padatkan keseluruhan garis lurus ke satu persamaan pendek — m beritahu anda kecerunan, c beritahu anda di mana ia melintasi paksi-y. Sebaik anda boleh baca persamaan itu, anda boleh ramal tepat di mana sebarang garis pergi, dan tepat di mana dua garis akan bertemu.

9.1

The Equation of a Straight LinePersamaan Garis Lurus

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Every straight line's equation can be written as y = mx + c. The number m is the gradient (how steep), and c is the y-intercept (where it crosses the y-axis). Lines with the SAME gradient never meet — they're parallel. Persamaan setiap garis lurus boleh ditulis sebagai y = mx + c. Nombor m ialah kecerunan (seberapa curam), dan c ialah pintasan-y (di mana ia melintasi paksi-y). Garis dengan kecerunan SAMA tidak pernah bertemu — ia selari.

Equation of a Straight LinePersamaan Garis Lurus
y = mx + c
m= gradient, c= y-intercept
Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Determine the gradient and y-intercept of: (a) y=2x+9 (b) 3y=−2x+12Tentukan kecerunan dan pintasan-y bagi: (a) y=2x+9 (b) 3y=−2x+12
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Line A is y=4x−1. Line B is y=4x+7. Are they parallel? Explain.Garis A ialah y=4x−1. Garis B ialah y=4x+7. Adakah ia selari? Jelaskan.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
Yes — both have gradient 4. Only the y-intercepts differ, so they never cross. Ya — kedua-dua ada kecerunan 4. Hanya pintasan-y berbeza, jadi tidak pernah bersilang.
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Reading the gradient straight off an equation like 3y=−2x+12 without dividing through first.Baca kecerunan terus daripada persamaan seperti 3y=−2x+12 tanpa bahagi dahulu.
✅Always rearrange into y=mx+c FIRST (divide every term by the coefficient of y) — only then read off m and c.Sentiasa susun semula ke y=mx+c DAHULU (bahagi setiap sebutan dengan pekali y) — hanya kemudian baca m dan c.

9.1

Point of Intersection of Two Straight LinesTitik Persilangan Dua Garis Lurus

📖 The IdeaIdea Utama

Two non-parallel lines cross at exactly ONE point — the only (x,y) that satisfies BOTH equations at once. Solve the two equations together (substitution or elimination) to find it, without needing to draw anything. Dua garis tidak selari bersilang pada tepat SATU titik — satu-satunya (x,y) memenuhi KEDUA-DUA persamaan serentak. Selesaikan kedua-dua persamaan bersama (gantian atau penghapusan) untuk cari, tanpa perlu lukis apa-apa.

Worked ExampleContoh Berpandu
Find the point of intersection of 2x+y=5 and x+2y=1.Cari titik persilangan 2x+y=5 dan x+2y=1.
🤝 Try It YourselfCuba Sendiri

Find the point of intersection of y=x+2 and y=3x−4.Cari titik persilangan y=x+2 dan y=3x−4.

👁️ Show AnswerTunjuk Jawapan
x+2=3x−4 → 6=2x → x=3. y=3+2=5. Point: (3,5).
⚠️ Watch OutBerhati-hati
❌Solving for x, then forgetting to substitute back to find y.Selesaikan untuk x, kemudian terlupa gantikan semula untuk cari y.
✅A point of intersection needs BOTH coordinates — always substitute your x (or y) back into one of the original equations to find the other.Titik persilangan perlukan KEDUA-DUA koordinat — sentiasa gantikan x (atau y) anda semula ke satu persamaan asal untuk cari yang satu lagi.
